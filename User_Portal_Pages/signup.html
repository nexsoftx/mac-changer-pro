Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions About Mac Changer Pro

Can I log into the Desktop Software right after creating a free account? ▼

Creating a free User Portal account lets you access your dashboard, order packages, and chat with Admin Support. However, Desktop Software login remains locked (`Unsubscribed`) until you purchase and activate a subscription package.

Why does Mac Changer Pro use a Setup Installer instead of a raw portable .exe? ▼

Single-file portable executables unpack binaries into %TEMP% on every launch, causing slow startup and false-positive antivirus flags. Our Setup Installer deploys pre-extracted binaries into %LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\Mac_Changer_Pro for instant startup and clean Windows Defender trust.

How do I customize the number of PCs on 6-Month, 1-Year, or Lifetime packages? ▼

Visit the Pricing & Packages page and click any of the customizable plans. Each plan opens its own dedicated customizer page (#/package-6month, #/package-yearly, #/package-lifetime) where you can increase or decrease the device count from 1 PC to 10 PCs with automatic price calculation.
